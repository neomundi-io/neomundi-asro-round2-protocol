# NeoMundi / ASRO — Round 2 Explicit Non-Claims

**Version:** v1
**Status:** Draft for ASRO review
**Scope:** Explicit exclusions and interpretation boundaries

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what NeoMundi / ASRO Round 2 does not establish.

These exclusions apply to:

* the protocol;
* the evidence record;
* the Reviewer report;
* future communications;
* public documentation;
* client-facing interpretation.

The absence of an explicit exclusion must not be interpreted as permission to extend the protocol claims.

---

## 2. No certification claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* ASRO certifies NeoMundi;
* NeoMundi certifies ASRO;
* the Reviewer certifies NeoMundi;
* the Reviewer certifies ASRO;
* any artefact constitutes a certification;
* one Round 2 execution constitutes an industrial certification;
* one successful review constitutes a commercial approval.

---

## 3. No mutual-validation claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* ASRO validates the NeoMundi runtime behavioral signal;
* NeoMundi validates ASRO governance-state outputs;
* NeoMundi measurement outputs validate ASRO witnessing outputs;
* ASRO witnessing outputs validate NeoMundi measurement outputs;
* the Reviewer converts distinct artefact classes into a single validation judgement.

NeoMundi outputs and ASRO outputs may appear within the same evidence record, but they remain evidence of distinct facts.

---

## 4. No universal-validity claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* the NeoMundi measurement model is universally valid;
* the measured signals explain all AI system behaviors;
* the measured signals apply identically to every model, provider, architecture or deployment context;
* the measured signals constitute a complete theory of AI behavior;
* one protocol execution proves general scientific validity;
* Round 2 constitutes final industrial validation.

---

## 5. No truth-adjudication claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* NeoMundi measures semantic truth;
* NeoMundi measures governance-state truth;
* ASRO adjudicates runtime behavioral truth;
* ASRO adjudicates semantic truth;
* the Reviewer adjudicates model truth;
* the Reviewer adjudicates infrastructure truth beyond the preserved evidence record.

---

## 6. No regulatory-compliance claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* NeoMundi guarantees regulatory compliance;
* ASRO guarantees regulatory compliance;
* the Reviewer guarantees regulatory compliance;
* the protocol proves compliance with any specific law, regulation, standard or certification framework;
* a Round 2 evidence record is sufficient on its own to satisfy all audit, procurement or legal requirements.

The protocol may support governance and due-diligence discussions, but it does not replace legal, regulatory or compliance assessment.

---

## 7. No infrastructure-certification claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* NeoMundi certifies ASRO infrastructure;
* ASRO certifies NeoMundi infrastructure;
* the Reviewer certifies the infrastructure used during execution;
* the protocol guarantees the absence of infrastructure defects;
* the protocol guarantees the absence of implementation defects;
* the protocol guarantees the absence of custody-chain defects.

---

## 8. No model-ranking claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* one AI model is globally superior to another;
* one provider is globally superior to another;
* a runtime measurement result constitutes a leaderboard;
* a runtime measurement result constitutes a rating;
* a runtime measurement result constitutes a commercial recommendation;
* a runtime measurement result constitutes a definitive model-selection decision.

---

## 9. No completeness claim

Round 2 does not establish that:

* the evidence record captures every relevant event;
* the protocol captures every possible failure mode;
* the protocol identifies every possible source of inconsistency;
* the protocol eliminates all ambiguity;
* the protocol replaces future validation work;
* the current metric set is final.

---

## 10. No extension beyond scope

Round 2 conclusions must not be extended beyond:

* the frozen protocol version;
* the defined execution context;
* the preserved artefacts;
* the explicit role boundary;
* the known limitations;
* the admissible runtime claims.

Any broader interpretation requires separate evidence, separate documentation and, where applicable, a new protocol version.
