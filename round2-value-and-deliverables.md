# NeoMundi / ASRO — Round 2

## Strategic Value, Deliverables and Reuse

**Status:** Working note
**Purpose:** Clarify the operational, methodological and commercial value of Round 2 without extending the protocol claims.

---

## 1. What Round 2 is

Round 2 is a specification and independent reviewability exercise.

Its purpose is to document the conditions under which a third-party Reviewer can examine a runtime AI measurement process using sealed artefacts, while preserving a strict separation between:

* NeoMundi runtime behavioral measurement;
* ASRO governance-state witnessing;
* independent post-hoc review.

Round 2 is not a certification exercise and does not imply that one party validates the operational role of the other.

---

## 2. Core boundary

The protocol is governed by the following role boundary:

* NeoMundi measures runtime behavior.
* ASRO witnesses the reviewability conditions around the measurement process.
* The Reviewer compares sealed artefacts after execution.
* No layer absorbs or certifies the role of another layer.

This separation must remain explicit in every protocol section, artefact category and future communication.

---

## 3. Expected deliverables

Round 2 is expected to produce:

1. A frozen and versioned protocol.
2. A clearly documented role-boundary model.
3. Witness constraints for each artefact class.
4. A list of admissible evidence categories.
5. A bounded comparison procedure for the independent Reviewer.
6. A formal freeze and signature procedure.
7. A sealed evidence record.
8. A Phase C independent review report.
9. A reusable documentation package for future due diligence.
10. A contribution paper authored by James after completion of Round 2.

---

## 4. Value for future clients

The Round 2 documentation package may be reused as a trust and due-diligence asset for future clients.

It can help a client understand:

* what NeoMundi measures;
* what NeoMundi does not claim to measure;
* how evidence is generated and sealed;
* how responsibilities are separated;
* how a third party can review the process;
* how protocol versions are frozen;
* how amendments are handled;
* which conclusions are admissible and which conclusions remain explicitly excluded.

The commercial value of Round 2 is therefore not limited to a single experiment. Its value lies in the creation of a reusable governance specification layer around the NeoMundi measurement instrument.

---

## 5. Commercial interpretation

Round 2 can reduce friction during:

* enterprise qualification;
* procurement review;
* governance discussions;
* compliance review;
* technical due diligence;
* partner onboarding;
* investor review.

It should be presented as evidence of methodological discipline and reviewability, not as a certification or universal validation.

---

## 6. Non-claims

Round 2 does not establish that:

* ASRO certifies NeoMundi;
* ASRO validates the runtime behavioral signal;
* NeoMundi certifies ASRO infrastructure;
* NeoMundi measures governance-state truth;
* the protocol proves regulatory compliance;
* the protocol proves universal validity of the measurement model;
* one successful execution constitutes full industrial validation.

---

## 7. Long-term reuse

The Round 2 package should become the first reusable specification asset in a broader NeoMundi governance documentation stack.

Future versions may support:

* client-specific measurement deployments;
* governance-layer integration;
* audit memoranda;
* procurement documentation;
* technical appendices;
* independent review procedures;
* contributor research;
* future specification work.
